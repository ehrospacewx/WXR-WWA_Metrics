How it's scored
  • Duplicates. Records are keyed on Bifrost's historyId; when the same product appears twice, the highest revision is kept.
  • Hits and false alarms come from Bifrost's own verification of each forecast product (verificationStatus). Hit ≥ desired lead = MET_DESIRED_LEAD_TIME, short lead = POSITIVE_LEAD_TIME, late = NEGATIVE_LEAD_TIME (event before issue). FA_SUB_THRESHOLD counts as a false alarm and is shown hatched.
  • Lead time = occurred time − issue time, which reproduces Bifrost's lead-time categories. Desired lead is read from each criterion (30 min for watches and strong-wind warnings).
  • Lightning watch POD. Each Phase 2 warning is an observed event at its location. It is covered if a Phase 1 watch for the same location was issued before it and had not expired or been cancelled. POD = covered ÷ events. Misses = uncovered events. CSI = hits ÷ (hits + misses + false alarms).
  • Strong-wind warnings. Bifrost exports record no missed wind events, so POD and CSI for wind need tower peak-wind data and are left blank; FAR and lead time are scored.
  • Observed products (wind advisories, Phase 2 warnings) aren't forecasts, so they're counted by time in effect rather than scored.
  • All times are UTC. Forecaster names are not stored or shown.
